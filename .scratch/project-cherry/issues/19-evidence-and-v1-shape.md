# Does the evidence change the v1 shape?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05

## Question

The time-estimation evidence research challenges two things settled while charting. Decide, with the author:

1. **Positioning.** There is no direct evidence the estimate → felt → actual loop trains time sense in ADHD. Does the app still present itself as training time awareness, or as "see your real numbers" (an external record that compensates rather than cures)? This shapes wording, the insights screen, and what success means.
2. **Depth of the similar-task hint.** The best-supported mechanism is showing the real past number at the moment of the next estimate for a similar task. That is the feature currently scoped "very thin". Should it move up — e.g. always shown at capture when a category has history — and does category alone remain the similarity key?
3. **Felt-time guess.** One lab study supports it, none in ADHD. Keep as designed, make optional, or cut?

Changing any of these edits the standing constraints in the map's Notes.
